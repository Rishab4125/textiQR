# TextiQR
## About
The textile and apparel industry faces a huge problem due to duplicates and counterfeits, which cause issues like trustworthiness in the product and authenticity. QR codes, for authentication purposes, are widely used in the market to provide transparency and product information. In textile industry, QR codes are mostly printed on tags which are then attached to the product/ clothes. Being easily replaceable and highly replicity there is always risk for tag alteration hence very less trust in current methodology.

I am research on use of directly printed or woven QR codes on clothes/ fabrics, providing more reliability and creating more enriching experience for customers. Alothoug this sound amazing there is a huge problem in normal case there are many obsticles related to scanning and reading the QR code directed printed on fabrics, some of which are due to geometric and perspective distortion due to the deforming and strechy nature of the clothes, thread profile of the fabric, shadow, noises, etc. Because of these challenges, in normal senario mobile camera or classic QR scanner is unable to scan QR printed on clothes.

TextiQR utilises exhaustive image preprocessing like filter, thresholding, Thine Plate Spline perspective correction and YOLO based object segmentation model (ERIC CANAS) to overcome the situation and provide state of the art QR code sacnner on textile.
